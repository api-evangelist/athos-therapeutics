Laurence retired from Goldman Sachs at the end of 2023 after 27 years of service at the firm. Laurence was the Chief Operating Officer and Executive Vice President of Goldman’s Asset & Wealth Management business from January 2022 to December 2023. He was also a member of the Management Committee.
